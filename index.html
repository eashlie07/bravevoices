BRAVE VOICES MUSICAL THEATER · AGES 7–10

Four afternoons, one show.

Kids learn stage vocabulary, build choreography, put together one ensemble number as a cast, and each polish a short solo with one-on-one coaching.

No experience needed, and no audition. We work as a group for energy and support, then carve out individual time so every kid has a piece that is genuinely theirs. On the last afternoon, families come and watch what they built.

Class is small on purpose — fifteen kids maximum.

WHY BRAVE VOICES PLAY · BELONG · GROW

Theater should feel like a place you can belong.

Some kids walk into a theater class already dreaming about the spotlight. Some are nervous. Some are hilarious at home and suddenly shy around strangers. All of them deserve a place in the room.

01

PLAY

Theater games make room for imagination, silliness, experimentation, and the freedom to try again.

02

BELONG

Kids don't have to arrive confident or experienced. They can be exactly where they are and grow from there.

03

GROW

Along the way, students practice listening, teamwork, creativity, communication, and finding their voice.

ABOUT THE PERSON BEHIND THE CLASS

HI, I'M ELIO.

I just want kids to have a place to do theater.

Brave Voices Academy was created because I believe theater should be available to young people right here in our community — not only to kids who already know they want to be performers.

My goal is simple: create classes where kids can play, experiment, make friends, learn real theater skills, and leave feeling a little braver than when they walked in.

FAQ FOR PARENTS

Questions parents actually have.

My child has never done theater. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Classes are designed to welcome beginners as well as kids with previous experience. No audition, ever.

What if my child is shy?

That's okay too. Kids are never required to walk in already confident. Theater games give students small, playful ways to participate and build comfort over time — nobody is put on the spot.

What should my child wear or bring?

Clothes they can move in and closed-toe shoes. Bring a water bottle. No costumes, no scripts, nothing to buy.

Do parents stay or drop off?

Drop off and pick up. On the last day of a class, families are invited to come in and watch what the kids built.

What happens if the class doesn't get enough sign-ups?

Classes need a minimum of five kids to run. If it doesn't fill, I'll tell you as early as I can and the class is cancelled rather than run short. Parks & Recreation refunds registration fees for a cancelled class.

Where does the class meet?

We usually meet in one of two places: the Parks & Recreation classroom in Woodland Park, or the Ute Pass Cultural Center. Parks & Recreation handles the room booking.

Where do I register, and what does it cost?

Registration goes through Woodland Park Parks & Recreation, either online or by phone at 719-687-5225. The fee listed on each class covers the whole course.

What if we have to miss a session?

Tell me beforehand and I'll make sure your child isn't behind. Each class is built so a missed afternoon doesn't break the final piece.
